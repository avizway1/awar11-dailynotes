Management Account : The account where we have enabled the "AWS organisation" feature 
- We take a dedicated account and we enable this feature. 
- We don't run any workload on this management account. 
- The management account gets the centralised billing for all the member accounts.
- We have an option to apply SCP (Service Control Policies) for permissions enforcement.
- We can integrate AWS Organisations with IAM Identity Centre for central logging and user management. 

** Management account can't be changed after enabling organisation.

OU : Organisational unit : Group of accounts which has similar characteristics.. 

SCP : service control policies : SCP is a maximum permission boundary for AWS accounts in an organisation. 
- It never grants access. 
- It limits what IAM users can do in Member accounts, even if they have admin access. 
- SCPs do not affect the management account. 
- SCP DOES affect the root user of member accounts.
