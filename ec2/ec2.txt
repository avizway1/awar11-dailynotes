Basically a server is a powerful computer that serves information, files, or services to other computers. How a server works:
1. The request: You click a link, check your email, or open an application.
2. The delivery: Your device sends a digital request over the internet to the server.
3. The response: The server processes a request then it sends the response back to your device.


The commonly used servers are:
- web servers
- game servers
- file servers
- mail servers
- Database Server
- Caching Server

Why can't we use our laptop/low hoardware as a server?
1. Servers never sleep. They are designed to run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without crashing.
2. They are built for huge traffic handling. A laptop can handle one user or ten users but a server handles thousands of users at the exact same time.

2. Reserved Instance : 
--> Commitment for 1yr / 3 yrs.. You get big discount in the pricing.
--> This is best for a steady predictable workload or an always-on kind of application. 

Reserved instances we have two options:
1. Standard reserved instance: This will be locked to a specific instance type or region with a good discount.
2. Convertible reserved instance: We can change the instance family/size at a slightly lower discount than a standard reserved instance but we have that flexibility to change the configuration.

Three pricing options in reserved instances:
1. Full upfront: pay 100% of the amount and you get your instance.
2. Partial upfront: pay 30-60% of the amount now then AWS will reduce the hourly price, you pay based on the reduced hourly price.
3. No upfront: pay everything on a usage basis.

** We can sell our reserved instances in AWS Marketplace, if we dont want it anymore. 

---------

3. Savings Plan : Savings Plans are a flexible pricing model that offer low prices on AWS usage, in exchange for a commitment to a consistent amount of usage (measured in $/hour) for a 1- or 3-year term.

Compute Savings Plans : Applies to EC2 instance usage, AWS Fargate, and AWS Lambda service usage, regardless of Region, instance family, size, tenancy, and operating system.

EC2 Instance Savings Plans : Applies to instance usage within the committed EC2 family and region, regardless of size, tenancy, and operating system.

Database Savings Plans : Applies to Aurora, RDS, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Amazon DocumentDB, Neptune, Amazon OpenSearch Service, and other services regardless of supported engine, instance family, size, deployment option, or Region.

SageMaker Savings Plans : Applies to SageMaker service usage, regardless of Region, instance family, and component.
